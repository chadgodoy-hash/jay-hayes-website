About Jay

From social work to the gut-brain connection.

I’m a psychotherapist and clinical hypnotherapist based in Brisbane, Australia. My work focuses on one thing: helping people whose digestive symptoms have taken over their lives.

My path

How I got here

My career began as an accredited social worker in demanding settings: corrections, drug and alcohol rehabilitation, and Indigenous mental health. That work taught me how much stress and lived experience shape the body, and how often people are left to manage both on their own.

Over time I found my true passion in psychotherapy, and in particular in working with clients living with digestive health disorders. So many had been through test after test and diet after diet, and still felt their gut was in charge.

Today I lead gut-brain coaching at Diet vs Disease, and I see clients one-to-one through my own practice. I bring together talk therapy, gut-directed hypnotherapy, nervous system work and EMDR, because the connection between the gut and the brain runs in both directions, and the best results come from working with both.
