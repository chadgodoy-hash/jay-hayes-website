Sound familiar?

You’ve done the tests. You’ve tried the diets. Your gut still runs the show.

  • Planning your day around where the nearest bathroom is
  • Pain, bloating or urgency that flares when life gets stressful
  • Eating less and less because so many foods feel risky
  • Being told your results are “normal” when you feel anything but
  • Worrying about symptoms, which seems to make them worse
  • Cancelling plans, travel or work because of your gut

None of this means it’s in your head. It means your gut and brain have got stuck in a loop, and that loop can be retrained.

How gut-brain therapy works

Your gut and brain talk all day long.

They’re connected by the vagus nerve and a dense network of nerves in the gut itself. Messages travel both ways, constantly.

After a long stretch of stress or symptoms, that line can become oversensitive. Ordinary digestion starts to register as pain or urgency, and the worry that follows sends even more alarm signals back down.

Gut-brain therapy works on that communication directly, helping your nervous system turn the volume down. It’s backed by decades of clinical research and is recommended in gastroenterology guidelines for IBS.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do I need a diagnosis first?

It helps. Gut-brain therapy suits functional gut conditions like IBS, which are diagnosed once other causes have been ruled out. If you haven’t seen a doctor about your symptoms yet, please start there.

Does this mean my symptoms are “all in my head”?

No. Your symptoms are real and physical. This therapy works with the nerve pathways that connect your gut and brain, which is where much of that sensitivity is set.

Is hypnotherapy like stage hypnosis?

Not at all. You stay aware and in control the whole time. It feels like deep, focused relaxation, and you can stop whenever you like.

Do I have to change my diet?

Not necessarily. Therapy can sit alongside dietary approaches like low FODMAP, and for some people it makes food feel less risky over time.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. We’ll talk about a likely timeframe in your first session and review it as we go.

Where do sessions happen?

Online, by video call, so you can join from home wherever you live.
